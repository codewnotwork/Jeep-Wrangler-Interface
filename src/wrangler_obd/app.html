Connect to the truck Key on

Plug the OBDLink into the port under the dash (left of the steering column) and into this laptop, then turn the key to ON.

Records every reading the truck supports. Connect first.
Drag a tile to move it. Use × to remove it.

Likely causes

Start monitoring. Patterns across the readings will be checked every few seconds.

1Pedal map Key on, engine off

Takes 45 seconds. After pressing Start, slowly press the gas pedal to the floor and let it back up, about 10 seconds each way, until the time runs out. If the truck doesn't report the pedal, that's fine: skip to step 2.

2Record a drive Start the engine first

Start the engine, press Start, then drive. Include a few minutes of idling, some town driving and some steady highway. Don't look at the laptop while driving: it stops on its own.

3Idle checks Engine warm, parked

Right after the drive. Each one takes 3 minutes. Set the truck up as described, press Start, and keep your feet off the gas.

A. In Park, A/C off, stereo off
B. In Drive, foot firmly on the brake
C. In Park, A/C on
D. In Park, stereo on loud

4Transmission temperature Key on, engine off

Do the drive first. The drive report tries to read transmission temperature directly.

Hot scan: right after a drive, engine off, key on. About 1 to 2 minutes.
Cold scan: the next morning before driving, engine off, key on.

5Send the results

Makes one ZIP file of everything recorded and opens its folder. Attach that file in the Claude thread.
